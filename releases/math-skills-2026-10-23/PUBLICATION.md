# Public distribution notes / 公开整理说明

This 2026-10-23 bundle publishes three complementary instruction skills: writing W35, reasoning R37 and the unnumbered math-paper workflow. Its purpose is clearer AI-assisted mathematical exposition without changing supplied mathematical content or proof logic. It makes no proof-certification or readability-performance guarantee.

## Preserved

- Stable rule IDs, complete operational fields, applicability and exclusion conditions, examples, Confidence qualifications, historical L1/L2/L3 labels and merge/candidate status.
- Separation of mathematical correctness from prose quality and mathematical preservation from a complete correctness audit.
- Task boundaries: pure copy-editing and translation do not automatically expand into research, proof review or training.
- Manual, opt-in learning contracts, source-exposure honesty, mathematical-before-writing review, and explicit limits on rule updates.
- Transferable mathematical checklists and language/organization guidance, without elevating historical observations into validated rules.

## Changed for distribution

- Private case-by-case evidence narratives are replaced by honest, bounded summaries. Raw learning manuscripts, conversations, detailed review transcripts, internal coordination records, personal identities/relationships and private locations are not published.
- In the reasoning cards HEAT-FK-M01 and SION-M03, narrow personalized-contract or private-review locator phrases inside Confidence are generalized. Their evidence levels, mathematical conditions, non-strong status and other confidence qualifications are unchanged. All six operational fields remain verbatim; all other Confidence fields remain verbatim.
- Personalized reference and reading-history details are replaced by neutral reference-selection guidance. No private relationship or source-upload provenance is retained.
- Cross-skill references are relative Markdown links. Host-specific optional UI files and unverified icon assets are excluded.
- Personal ongoing-task instructions are expressed as task-scoped, opt-in behavior. No executable training, automation, scripts, model weights or external-service credentials are supplied.
- Chinese and English usage documents, version status, scoped MIT license and integrity manifest are added. Prior release directories are not modified by this bundle.

## Evidence limitations

“Validated” and L1/L2/L3 labels are inherited source classifications under their original limitations, not validation performed during packaging. Private evidence records are excluded, so this bundle is not sufficient for independent reproduction of those labels or old/new version comparisons. Public bibliography links identify works; they do not reproduce private experiments, certify every cited page or make broader mathematical claims.

No human-reader study, statistical causal result, guaranteed correctness, model success rate, general version superiority, author endorsement or publication acceptance is claimed. A final audit can miss errors; successful compilation does not validate mathematics. One proof unit is not a whole-paper result. Chinese reconstruction of English sources is not validation of English drafting or Chinese original-publication style. Mathematical repairs and new calculations must be distinguished from pure editorial benefits.

The reasoning rules require current mathematical checks. They are not new universal theorems and cannot be applied without their hypotheses. Workflow style observations remain observations or baseline checklists unless independently supported at the recorded level. Source references and inherited page locators are not a fresh page-by-page mathematical audit by this distribution.

## Responsible use

Choose rules by their “Use when” and “Avoid when” fields. Preserve unverified status, provenance and copyright boundaries. Do not silently change assumptions, results or proof logic to produce smoother prose. Report a substantive mathematical concern separately and obtain the appropriate task scope before constructing a revised result. Do not claim unperformed checks, unseen source pages or unavailable historical records.

The package's file checks and rule-preservation comparisons concern documentation integrity only. They do not establish mathematical correctness or improved writing outcomes.
