# 公开证据说明 / Public evidence limits

This is a public packaging of the stable W35 writing instructions, not a new training run, benchmark, mathematical result, or rule upgrade. The underlying writing workflow was developed with AI-assisted reconstruction and review. This package does not claim human peer review, author endorsement, independent theorem discovery, or improved success rates.

## What is preserved

- MW-O001, MW-O002 and MW-O003 retain their scoped Level 3 / validated labels and original confidence qualifications. They are not labelled strong.
- KM86-W03 remains Level 2 / candidate. MW-O004 remains a historical merged candidate, not an extra validated rule.
- The six operational fields and Confidence fields are preserved verbatim. Stage ordering, mathematical-preservation requirements and language boundaries are retained.
- W36 is not included. No rule change from a later review is implied.

## What readers can and cannot verify

The bibliography identifies selected primary publications already present in the W35 evidence history. It is not a complete training-corpus manifest. Public links identify those works; they do not reproduce private comparisons or certify every cited mathematical statement.

The Evidence fields are shortened historical summaries. Detailed drafts, source-opening records, audit transcripts and per-round comparisons are not included. Consequently, the inherited L3 status cannot be independently reproduced from this release alone. Source page locators are inherited, not a claim of a fresh page-by-page verification during packaging.

The evidence supports conditional editorial choices only. A study unit is not a whole-paper validation. Comparing Chinese reconstructions with English sources does not establish English drafting quality or Chinese publication style. Additional mathematical proofs are not pure prose gains. A successful final audit does not imply a correct first draft, a zero-error system or a causal advantage for a newer skill version.

## Using the rules responsibly

Select rules by their Use when and Avoid when fields. Check the current mathematics independently. Treat counterexamples or new settings as reasons to narrow, split or downgrade a rule. Do not invent missing evidence, historical priority, experiments, readership measurements or author approval.

Private research materials and copyrighted papers are not redistributed. Only bibliographic references are supplied. Preserve attribution when working from a paper and label learning reconstructions as such.
